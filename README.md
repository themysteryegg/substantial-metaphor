My name is Chandler. I am a writer and researcher. I give all my best ideas away for free.

Right now, I am working on several projects.

1) The Substantial Metaphor
2) The Unnamed trilogy
3) The Mystery Egg

The Substantial Metaphor is by far the most important of these works. It will be the next philosophical treatise. In a sense, the book will be a manual of interpretation. The philosophy it represents is totally novel. I have been working on it for about 8 years. It is approximately 33% finished by volume, and perhaps 50% finished by weight of idea. Its format is completed and most of the hard research has been done. It is presently in the manuscript stage. You can find its Table of Contents [here]().

The Unnamed is a science fiction trilogy I am writing, a project that began shortly after I started the SM project. I needed a place to shove all the ideas that research into virtually every subject of science (including physics, psychology, geology, chemistry, astronomy, and sociology) and philosophy (including language, logic, ethics, ontology, epistemology, religion, and history) produced that did not belong in the SM manual. Eventually, these random notes gained independent coherence, becoming characters, a setting, and a plot. My hope is that this work will help illustrate the logic of SM, to act as both a companion piece to the manual to demonstrate its arguments in a contained setting and to be independently entertaining. It is a story about the people of history who go unnamed whose actions really determine the fate of society. More materially, it is about a man and a woman who become the de facto leaders of all of humanity in the year ninety-nine trillion. Book 1: The Spear will be published in December of 2026.

The Mystery Egg is my blog, where I put the thoughts that fit no where else but with which I simply cannot dispense. Find it [here](https://substack.com/@mysteryegg).
